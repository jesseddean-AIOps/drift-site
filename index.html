Drift

A field journal for fly anglers.

Drift logs your day by voice, wraps every catch in the real conditions of that moment, and turns the whole thing into a journal that gets more useful every time you go. River flows come from USGS and state gauges and are compared to the historical record for that day of the year.

Made for anglers, not feeds.
