What Drift is

Drift is a fly fishing journal. It records what you tell it, shows public river and weather data, and uses AI services to write briefs, rank waters and compile journal drafts from your own notes.

Conditions data and AI content are information, not advice

River flows, lake levels, weather, snowpack, hatch calendars, daily intel and river briefs are assembled from public sources and AI models. They can be wrong, late, or missing. Gauges fail, provisional readings get revised, and a language model can state something confidently that is not true. Do not rely on Drift to judge whether water is safe to wade, float or cross. Check conditions yourself, respect posted closures and regulations, and use your own judgment on the water. You fish at your own risk.

Fishing regulations shown in the app are summaries and may be out of date. The state wildlife agency's current regulations control.
